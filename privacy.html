This policy covers Heat Daily Uploader ("the app"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Heat Daily channel.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's channel and to read the channel's own video statistics.
  • TikTok: basic account info, permission to publish videos and to read the owner's own video list and view counts.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the owner's professional account and to read their insights.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the owner's own videos and to track their views.
  • Access tokens and logs are stored locally on the owner's computer. No server or database collects them.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect personal data from viewers or other users.

Google / YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked from each platform's connected-apps settings. Deleting the local token files removes all stored access. To request deletion of any data, contact the address below.
